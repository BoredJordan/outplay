# Outplay privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Outplay plays short videos that people send you (Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts) with proper controls. It has no account, no feed and no servers of its own. The Google Play version shows one ad on the end screen, which a one-time purchase removes.

## What we collect

Outplay itself collects nothing. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data, and it has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting. There are two things to know. The video comes from the platform's own embedded player (see Network traffic), and that player can collect data of its own. And the Google Play version shows an ad (see Ads), served by Google, which collects data as described there.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (for example, whether videos play straight away or start with the sound on), a few small counters (how many videos you have finished and on how many days, which decide when the ad first appears and when to suggest a rating) and, if you buy it, a flag that ads are removed. These are kept in the app's own local storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app removes them.

There is also a hidden diagnostics log, for testing. It is off by default and records nothing unless someone unlocks it in Settings and switches it on. When on, it keeps, for each of the last 200 links you open in Outplay, the time, the link, and whether it played or why it didn't. It stays on your device, in the app's own storage, and is never sent anywhere by Outplay. The only way it leaves is if you choose Share log and pick where to send it. Switching it off or tapping Clear deletes it, and so does uninstalling the app.

## Network traffic

Outplay's own code makes no requests of its own, and it has no servers. The only traffic is the video platform's own player loading, as it would in a browser. For Instagram, TikTok and Facebook that is the platform's embed page. For YouTube it is YouTube's player and its player script, loaded into a small page inside Outplay. The player is run by the platform (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or YouTube), not by Outplay. Loading it sends the platform your IP address and device details such as browser version, and the platform may also set cookies or collect other data under its own privacy policy. Outplay does not receive, control or see any of that. If you would rather not load a platform's page, don't open its links in Outplay. Outplay does not log in for you and never sees your account details.

## Ads and the removal purchase

This applies to the Google Play version only. The web extension and any other builds show no ads and have no purchase.

After a video ends, the Google Play version shows one ad on the end screen. The first seven videos you finish show none. It is never shown before or during a video.

- **Who serves it.** The ad is served by Google AdMob, using Google's Mobile Ads SDK. To show it, the SDK sends a request to Google when a video starts playing, so the ad is ready at the end. Google may receive your IP address, device and app details, and may use an advertising ID or similar identifier where the ads allow it. What Google collects is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Outplay does not receive any of it.
- **Not personalised.** The ads are not personalised. Outside the UK and EEA they are non-personalised ads. In the UK and EEA they are Google's "limited ads", which use no device identifiers. Because of this, Outplay does not ask for consent, and shows no consent form. Outplay sets no interest or location targeting.
- **Not for children.** Outplay is not aimed at children, and its ads are not child-directed.
- **If this changes.** If personalised ads are ever switched on, this policy will be updated first and consent will be asked before any personalised ad is shown.

Removing the ad is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Outplay does not see your payment details. It asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made and keeps only an "ads removed" flag on your device. Sharing a video or rating Outplay sends nothing to Outplay either: the share sheet or the store page opens on your device, and what happens next is between you and the app or store you chose.

## YouTube

Outplay uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube IFrame Player API) to play YouTube videos. By playing a YouTube video in Outplay you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). YouTube's player may store or read cookies or similar technology on your device, and may show content or ads served by Google; Outplay does not control this. What it collects is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Outplay itself does not access, collect or store any information from YouTube.

## Links that leave the app

If you tap a rating or share link, it opens in your browser or store app. That is your action, and the page you land on is covered by its own policy.

## Children

Outplay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. It is not aimed at children, and its ads are not child-directed.
