# Outplay privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Outplay plays short videos that people send you (Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts) with proper controls. It has no account, no feed and no servers of its own.

## What we collect

Nothing. Outplay does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your device

Your settings (for example, whether videos open paused) and a few small counters that decide when to show a single one-time rating or tip suggestion. These are kept in the app's own local storage on your device. They are never sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app removes them.

## Network traffic

Outplay's own code makes no network requests. The only traffic is the video platform's own embed page loading, as it would in a browser. That page is run by the platform (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or YouTube) and is covered by that platform's own privacy policy. Outplay does not log in for you and never sees your account details.

## Links that leave the app

If you tap a rating or tip link, it opens in your browser or store app. That is your action, and the page you land on is covered by its own policy.

## Children

Outplay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
